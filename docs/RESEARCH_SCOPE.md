# Research Scope

## Questions
1. Can temporal context improve surgical phase recognition under occlusion?
2. How do segmentation errors propagate into 3D surface reconstruction?
3. Which spectral-spatial models remain stable under band dropout and illumination shift?
4. How should stereo confidence and calibration residuals gate 3D outputs?
5. Can explicit failure-state decision trees make experiments easier to audit?

## Out of scope
- diagnosis or treatment recommendation;
- autonomous surgical control;
- claims of clinical efficacy;
- storing real patient data in this repository.

## Recommended reporting
Use task-specific metrics plus calibration/uncertainty, stratify by failure conditions, and include qualitative error galleries.
